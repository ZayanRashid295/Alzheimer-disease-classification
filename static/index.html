About this tool

Research use only.

This classifier predicts cognitive stage from brain MRI using a custom CNN trained on labeled datasets. Typical classes include NonDemented, VeryMildDemented, MildDemented, and ModerateDemented (exact names depend on the trained checkpoint).

Grad-CAM

“Show explanation” overlays a heatmap on your image indicating which regions the model focused on for its prediction. Red areas correspond to higher influence.

Disclaimer: This tool is for research and education only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Always consult qualified healthcare providers.
